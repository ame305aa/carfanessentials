The range

Packages drawn from one mark.

Oils, creams, soaps, and shampoo share a label: white ground, a whisper of branches, the tree in its circle, and a spine that tells you the family. Ask the workshop and we will tell you what is on the bench.

How to read a Carfan pack

The same bones on every essential.

White, then branches

The ground is white so the mark stays clear. A pale branch sits at the top of the carton, a quiet reminder of the tree without crowding the name.

A spine in the family color

Resin brown for oils. Gold for creams. Bark for soaps. The name is set vertically on that spine, the way the brand book sets lemongrass.

The tree, and Jigjiga

The circle and the wordmark sit on the face. Under them, a small line: made in Jigjiga. If the tree is missing, it is not our pack.

Resin evening

Frankincense warmed with a soft, sweet note for the end of the day. Packed in amber glass so the oil stays quiet on a shelf. The label is the brand book’s amber bottle, with our evening name on the spine when the batch is this one.

  • Gold-brown side band
  • Rested longer than the daytime oils
